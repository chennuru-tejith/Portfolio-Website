About

Hi, I'm Tejith.

I'm an integrated BBA (Hons.) + MBA student at O.P. Jindal Global University, from Hyderabad. I like work where the numbers have to hold up: tracking a CSR budget on site, analysing five years of employee costs, keeping vendor records audit-ready.

On campus I head outreach for ZAMAC, sit on university committees and directed a 200+ member entrepreneurship club. Alongside the degree I build my own tools, and I'm writing a research paper on how AI search decides what to cite.
